05. Sample Story Activity

Weekend Detective Game

A storytelling activity where students share three sentences about their weekend. Two are true. One is a lie. Friends ask questions to find the lie.

1. What is it?

Students tell a short weekend story using simple past tense. The class listens carefully and becomes detectives.

2. Sentence Rules

  • Write three clear sentences.
  • Two sentences are true.
  • One sentence is a lie.
  • Use past simple tense.
  • Tell the story in order.

3. Example

Student A:

  1. I went to the cinema on Saturday.
  2. I played pickleball for two hours.
  3. Then I bought milk tea.

Friends ask questions to find which sentence is the lie.

4. How to Play

  1. Write your sentences.
  2. Read them aloud.
  3. Ask follow-up questions.
  4. Vote for the lie.
  5. Reveal the truth.
