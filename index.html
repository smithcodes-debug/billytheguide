Diving in Phu Quoc Island

If you are open water diver ? 4 to 10M ? no doubt - this's right fit for you.

Diving in Phu Quoc is not just about seeing beautiful corals — it is about discovering one of Vietnam’s rich marine ecosystems, connected to the wider Indo-Pacific biodiversity region. Around Phu Quoc’s small islands, especially in the south, divers can find diverse coral reefs, reef fish, sponges, and other marine life. The best experience depends on local knowledge: choosing the right reef, the right season, and the right water conditions for a safe and meaningful dive.

Leave no trace

Leave no trace is the travel promise behind Billy and Friend Adventures: enjoy Phú Quốc without damaging coral reefs, beaches, forest paths, or the quiet local places that make the island worth visiting.

Guests are guided to move slowly, avoid touching coral, reduce waste, respect local routes, and leave each place as natural as possible for the people who arrive after them.

FAQ
Are these private experiences?
Yes. The focus is on private, slower, and more personal Phú Quốc experiences instead of crowded standard tours.
Is snorkeling beginner-friendly?
Yes. The snorkeling experience is designed to be calm, guided, and respectful of coral safety.
How does the tour protect coral reefs?
The guide explains safe movement, no-touch reef behavior, cleaner travel habits, and why coral needs distance and respect.
