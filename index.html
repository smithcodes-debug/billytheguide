Billy and Friend Adventures

Private Phu Quoc experiences with a local guide

"Some laugh because we are different.
We smile because we choose not to be the same."

We choose a slower, a more personal way to discover Phú Quốc through coral-friendly snorkeling or diving, with quiet island camping sometimes, and live a life shaped by a Leave no trace mindset.that's it.

No crowd

Phu Quoc has many beautiful snorkeling corners, and a good day does not need to follow the crowd. Before your trip, Billy and Friend studies the weather, water, timing, and island mood to choose quieter places for you. We do the planning, checking, and adjusting, so your family can simply arrive, breathe, and enjoy the reef in a calmer, more personal, unhurried way.

Underwater guide

Our underwater guide does not watch from far away. We swim beside you, help you move slowly, keep distance from coral, and notice small risks before they become problems. When the water is right, we also guide your eyes to the better reef angles, fish movement, and quiet beauty that many visitors miss around Phu Quoc beneath the surface.

Kid + disabled friendly

At Billy and Friend, every guest deserves a safe and happy way to join the sea. We plan slower routes, easier entry points, extra rest time, and closer guide support for children, older travelers, and guests with disabilities or special needs. The goal is not to push everyone the same way, but to help everyone feel included, understood, and protected.

Clean professional reef gear

Clear reef viewing starts with equipment that is clean, well checked, and comfortable to use. We prepare professional snorkeling gear, keep masks and accessories in good condition, and match equipment carefully before entering the water. For guests who need vision support, we can prepare separate viewing options, so the reef experience feels calmer, clearer, easier, and more personal to enjoy.

Current controlled

Current changes can turn a beautiful snorkeling spot into the wrong place at the wrong time. Billy and Friend uses local experience, daily observation, and our own water condition forecast to plan safer reef choices. We look at wind, current, visibility, tide, and guest ability before entering the water, helping you avoid unnecessary risk and enjoy coral with more confidence.

Media with you

A sea day becomes more meaningful when the small moments come home with you. During the trip, when conditions are suitable, we help record natural memories of your family, your first reef view, quiet island time, and the joy after swimming. The focus is not staged perfection, but honest travel moments you can keep, share, remember, and smile about later.

No extra fee

After snorkeling, simple island comfort can feel better than anything fancy. Seasonal tropical fruit, fresh juice, and small refreshments are included without an extra fee, depending on the day and local availability. You can come out of the water, slow down, taste something fresh from Phu Quoc, and let the body rest peacefully before the journey continues.

Flexible pickup and cancellation

We want the trip to feel easy before it even starts. Pickup can be arranged from almost anywhere in Phu Quoc, depending on your location and the daily route. Plans can also change, especially with weather and family travel. That is why guests may cancel up to three days before departure, giving more comfort, flexibility, and less pressure.

Handmade ocean gift

After the tour, we want you to carry home more than photos. Billy and Friend prepares a small handmade souvenir from Phu Quoc, created with respect for the ocean and our Leave no trace spirit. Some gifts are inspired by marine cleanup and recycled sea waste, turning what should not be in the water into a reminder to protect it.

Phu Quoc Coral Dictionary

Simple notes to help guests understand, respect, and protect reef life.

The total area of coral reefs zoned for conservation in Phu Quoc is approximately 473.9 hectares.

An Thoi Archipelago (The South): Known as the coral "capital" of Phu Quoc, it accounts for over 76% of the total area (roughly 362.2 hectares). This area also boasts the highest coral cover density and serves as the strictly protected zone of the Marine Protected Area.

The North of the Island: Covers a significantly smaller area (about 111.7 hectares), scattered around locations such as Hon Mot and Ganh Dau Cape.

Note from regulatory authorities: Despite the high species diversity, Phu Quoc's coral reefs are currently under immense pressure from climate change (specifically coral bleaching due to rising sea temperatures), tourism development, and fishing activities. The average live coral cover is currently recorded at around 28.1%. Consequently, the Kien Giang provincial government is actively collaborating with scientific organizations to implement natural reef restoration projects.
